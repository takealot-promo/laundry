Description

Upgrade your kitchen and laundry room with the Hisense Laundry & Dishwasher Bundle. This complete 2-appliance home combo pairs the Hisense 13 Place Dishwasher with LED Display – Silver (Model H13DSS) with the high-capacity Hisense 15kg Top Loader Washing Machine with Aqua Save iBubble (Model WT3K1522UB).

Product 1: Hisense 13 Place Dishwasher with LED Display – Silver (Model H13DSS)

  • 13 Place Settings Capacity: Spacious 60cm freestanding dishwasher engineered to clean up to 13 place settings (over 140 individual items) in a single cycle.
  • A++ Energy Class & 11L Water Efficiency: Low energy consumption (0.93 kWh/cycle) and only 11 litres of water per cycle.
  • Electronic Push-Button Control with LED Display: Intuitive front panel with digital countdown timer and status indicators.
  • 8 Wash Programs & Hygiene Wash: Intensive (60°C), Eco (45°C), Light (45°C), Crystal (40°C), 60 Min (60°C), Rapid (40°C), Pre-Rinse, and Self-Clean (60°C).
  • 2-Level Adjustable Upper Basket: Easily adjust the upper rack height to fit tall pots, pans, and large serving dishes, complete with foldable spikes, 4 cup holders, and a cutlery basket.
  • Aqua Protect System & Power Off Memory: Built-in water overflow and low-water protection, Child Lock, Half Load (1/2) mode, and Power Off Memory that resumes your cycle automatically after power outages.

Product 2: Hisense 15kg Top Loader Washing Machine with Aqua Save iBubble (Model WT3K1522UB)

  • 15 kg Drum Capacity (85L Tub Volume): Easily handles king-size duvets, heavy blankets, curtains, and large family laundry loads in fewer cycles.
  • iBubble Technology: Powerful counter-clockwise water vortex dissolves detergent up to 150% more efficiently and generates dense foam that penetrates deep into fibres while saving up to 16% detergent.
  • Aqua Save Function: Retains final rinse water for reuse on the next load, reducing water consumption by up to 30%.
  • 8 Dedicated Wash Programs + Soak: Normal, Quick 15', Eco, Delicates, Intensive Wash, Air Dry, Spin, and Tub Clean.
  • Monolith Design & Soft-Closing PureFlat Glass Lid: Sleek Premium Black finish with a damped tempered glass lid that closes smoothly and silently.
  • Magic Filter & Smart Load Detection: Captures lint and fluff effectively even at low water levels, while intelligent sensors automatically match water levels to load weight.
  • Convenience & Safety: Pause & Add function, Child Lock, Memory Backup (auto-restart after power failure), Delay Start, and built-in Drain Pump.
